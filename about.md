# GDMenu Music Changer

Replaces the main menu music with something else. It comes with three original tracks, and you can use your own too.

Included tracks:

- Neon Runner: fast and upbeat
- Cube Drift: slow and chill
- Spike Pulse: fast and heavy

Pick one in the mod settings, or tap the Next song button in the top left of the main menu to switch right away. You can also set it to random, point it at your own mp3, ogg or wav file, or turn it off to get the normal menu music back.

Works on Windows and iOS. If you use your own music, make sure you're allowed to use it.
